[manifesto]

We romanticize the pause.

Love, in its most radical form, is not shouted. It is held. This space is an altar dedicated to Black intimacy: to care, to rest, and to the beauty of existing without haste. Our bodies have been designed for struggle, but they deserve rest. Like the rain that washes the city, like the neon that tints the night in pink and purple, love flourishes in stillness.
